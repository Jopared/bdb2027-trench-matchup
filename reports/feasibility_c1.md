## 1. G1: drill reliability (cross-stance / cross-side consistency)

**Important data finding (verified by code): the two reps are not identical-condition repeats.** Note from Kaggle discussion 751165 (host information, not verified by me): rep A is from a 3-point stance dropping right, rep B from a 2-point stance dropping left; task is drop to a 2-yd cone, open hips at 4 yd, finish at 10 yd. In the tracking, the chronologically first rep drifts toward -y and the second toward +y. I label sides purely by drift direction (sign of y_end - y_start), NOT by stance, since orientation/stance is not in the data; the mapping of drift sign to stance/left-right is the host's statement, not something I verified.

- Players with 2+ reps: 118; start distance between the two reps: median 0.50 yd, max 2.54 yd (all within 3 yd). 3 reps: player 57796 (first two used). Reps per player: 2 players have 1 rep, 117 have 2, 1 has 3.
- Side by drift: pairs with one rep of each side: **110 of 118** (93.2%); same-side pairs: 8. Chronological rep order vs drift: rep 1 drifts -y for 114/118, rep 2 drifts +y for 114/118 (so rep order is essentially confounded with side/stance).
- **candidates.md is wrong on this point:** it describes the 117 pairs as "genuine same-start repeats / true test-retest". They share a start point but not the movement: the reps are mirrored drops (different side, and per the host different stance). G1 here is therefore a cross-stance consistency, a lower bound on reliability.

## 8. Things candidates.md got wrong or that need qualification

1. **"Genuine test-retest / same-start repeats" is wrong in kind.** Pairs start together (median 0.5 yd apart) but the reps are mirrored drops (110 of 118 pairs, 93%, drift opposite ways in y [host said about 96%]; rep 1 drifts -y, rep 2 +y). Per the host (Kaggle discussion 751165, not verified here) they are also different stances (3-point right, 2-point left). The candidate's 'only skill drill with true repeats' claim and its Statistics-defensibility score of 5 should be revised: G1 is a cross-stance lower bound.
2. **Systematic side/stance effect.** Rep 2 vs rep 1 (3-frame): set_depth_1s +0.59 yd, peak_set_speed +0.80 yd/s, set_burst -0.94 s (rep 2 much quicker), set_lateral_drift +0.64 yd; all p < 1e-18 (table in section 1).
3. **Drill shape.** Direction convention OK: the drill runs toward -x (mean total dx = -10.4 yd) and the trajectory has the described slow phase 1 then acceleration (phase 2); but phase 1 is strongly lateral (Euclidean depth at 1 s = 1.4 yd vs 1.07 yd backward), and speed profile differs by side. Recording start is at s about 0.3-0.5 yd/s (already moving), so onset is at frame 0 in most 2024-25 reps and later in 2023.
4. **play_direction / x-axis convention is correct** in game tracking (verified: backward displacement positive for both left and right plays). Note game tracking has no frame_id; time is at 10 Hz and `ball_snap` is present for the plays used.
5. `lined_up_position` for OL is already 'T' / 'G' / 'C' (not LT/RT); TE/FB/WR rows can carry non-null pressure_allowed (about 200 cohort snaps) and were excluded. 195 pass-block snaps have pass_result == '' (non-dropback) and are kept (their pressure_allowed is non-null).
6. Outcome coverage 95.7% (document: 95.5%); pass-block snap counts agree within 1-2 players except >= 200 snaps.
7. Time-to-1yd and set_burst are threshold-crossing metrics sensitive to the recording crop; with the crop starting mid-motion some reps give set_burst of ~0.1 s.
