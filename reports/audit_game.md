# Audit of in-game data (player_play, games, game tracking, career)

Generated by `scripts/audit/audit_game.py` (re-run to reproduce every number). All figures below were computed by that script from `data/processed/*.parquet`; statements marked *inference* are interpretation.

**Conventions.** `REG` = games with `season_type == 'REG'`; `ALL` = every game in `games` (REG + POST + PRE). Position groups (from `players.nfl_position`, NOT from `lined_up_position`): WR, TE, RB_FB (RB+FB), OL (T,G,C), IDL (DT,NT), EDGE (DE,OLB), LB (ILB,MLB; only 2 players), CB, S (FS,SS), DB_generic (position `DB`). A 'row' is one player_play row (one player on one play). 'Dropback' = `pass_result <> ''` (see section H1: **empty string, not null**). Unless stated, qualifying plays exclude the `lined_up_position == 'NA'` pre-snap-penalty rows.

## Key findings (all numbers computed by this script; details in sections)

- **Exposure is highly unequal.** 497/510 players have any rows but only 401 have REG rows. REG rows by draft class: 2023 118,916, 2024 90,519, 2025 39,834 (median REG rows per cohort player 352 / 242 / 96). 20.7% of player_play rows are PRE or POST (16.5% PRE).
- **Dropback flag**: `pass_result` is the empty string (not null) on 103,636 rows; `pass_result <> ''` is the dropback test. `is_not_null()` (as the convert.py docstring says) selects every row.
- **Receiving** (REG routes): WR 68/106 players >=50 routes, 62 >=100, 49 >=200; TE 24/42, 22, 15; RB/FB essentially none (40 REG routes). Targets: WR 35 players >=50, 24 >=100; TE 13 >=50, 5 >=100. separation_at_pass_forward is populated on ~100% of routes on plays with a throw (C/I/IN) - targets AND non-targets - and 0% on sacks/scrambles (so ~85-88% of all routes); YAC/rec_yards only on catches; cushion ~95% WR, ~80% TE.
- **Pass protection** (pressure_allowed non-null): OL 70/121 players >=50 REG pass-block snaps, 66 >=100, 60 >=200 (ALL games: 103/89/68); TE/RB/WR pass blocks are negligible. Pressure-allowed rate 8.4%, sack allowed 1.15% of snaps. On run plays an OL row carries nothing beyond context (section B6).
- **Pass rush** (blitzing & dropback): EDGE 45/66 players >=50 REG rush snaps, 39 >=100, 27 >=200; IDL 34/52, 26, 15; CB/S/LB too few. Pressure is rare (EDGE 11%, IDL 7% of rush snaps): players with >=10 pressures: EDGE 33, IDL 18; total REG sacks EDGE 309, IDL 87.
- **Coverage** (real assignment = coverage_assignment non-null and not NO_ASSIGNMENT): CB 40/60 players >=50 REG snaps, 38 >=100, 33 >=200; S 31/53, 29, 26. A coverage row has the assignment (MAN or zone type) and `cushion`, but NO target, reception, separation-allowed, yards-allowed or completion-allowed for the defender; pass_result / yards_gained are play-level and identical for all players on the play. A defender outcome would have to come from pairing with a cohort receiver on the same play (opposing WR/TE-vs-DB pairs exist on 17,226 REG plays) or be derived from tracking.
- **Run defence**: tackle/assist/tackle_for_loss/caused_forced_fumble are populated only on credited rows (null = no credit; they share one null pattern); denominator = defensive snaps. REG run-defence snaps >=100: IDL 24, EDGE 34, CB 35, S 27; players with >=25 REG tackles: S 21, CB 27, EDGE 17, IDL 4.
- **Joins**: (game_id, play_id, nfl_id) is unique in player_play; every nfl_id and game_id resolves in players/games. 2,497 tracking keys lack a player_play row (all WR/TE/FB) and 1,750 player_play rows lack tracking (74% are NA pre-snap-penalty rows).
- **Season types**: games.csv includes PRE (147 games, 16.5% of player_play rows). Filter on games.season_type == 'REG' deliberately. All 5 generic-`DB` players appear only in PRE (career defensive snaps 0).
- **Tracking**: 0.1 s sampling (22,339,338 of 22,339,346 intervals, 8 gaps), no nulls in x/y/s/a/dis/o/dir, 0 duplicate frames; about 10 frames before the snap and about 10 after the last event; ball_snap on 99.8% of player-plays; event tags are play-level (same tag on every cohort player on that frame; never partial or conflicting); x/y are raw field coordinates (not normalised to offence direction); a small number of implausible speeds/accelerations and out-of-field positions (section D).
- **Career table**: career snaps equal REG player_play rows exactly for non-WR/TE groups; Spearman(draft pick, career primary-side snaps) = -0.63 over 383 drafted players (within class roughly -0.6 to -0.7). Honours are too rare to model: 6 All-Pro 1st team, 2 2nd team, 12 players with any All-Pro/Pro Bowl.
- **Surprises**: (i) no special-teams plays (no punts, kickoffs, FGs) in player_play; (ii) `nfl_position` is not the alignment (e.g. SS lines up as OLB/MLB/CB/EDGE, DT as EDGE); (iii) `game_clock` is minutes:seconds stored as a HH:MM time; (iv) `target` is never null (False for every defender; True on only 4 OL rows); (v) cohort-vs-cohort co-occurrence exists on 74% of tracked plays, so tracking is not 'one player per play'; (vi) PRE rows contain 78% of the NA pre-snap-penalty rows.
